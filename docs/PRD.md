# Admitra AI — Product Requirements Document

## Problem

Indian brands expanding beyond their home region face a fundamental content problem: they blast the same generic Hindi/English creative across all of India. This doesn't work because India isn't one market — it's dozens. Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka, Bengal, Maharashtra each have distinct languages, cultural references, festivals, aesthetics, and local identity.

Creating hyperlocal brand content that actually resonates requires deep regional knowledge — the right language, the right cultural context, and knowing what's locally famous in a specific town or region. A filter coffee reference lands in Coimbatore. A Durga Puja reference lands in Kolkata. A generic "festival sale" banner lands nowhere.

Today, doing this at scale means hiring regional agencies, local copywriters, translators, and designers per market. This is expensive, slow, and completely out of reach for small brands. The result: D2C brands and service businesses either skip regional markets entirely or run poorly localized campaigns that waste ad spend and fail to connect.

## Goal

Enable any brand — regardless of team size or budget — to generate hyperlocal, culturally resonant ad creatives across Indian regions in minutes, not weeks.

A single brief in, a complete regional creative package out.

## Target Audience

### Primary: D2C Brand Marketers

**Priya — Marketing Manager at a D2C skincare brand (Series A)**

Priya runs a 3-person marketing team. They've grown well in metro cities through Instagram and Facebook ads in English and Hindi. Now the founder wants to expand into South India and Bengal. Priya knows her current creatives won't work — she's seen the low engagement numbers in non-Hindi regions. She's looked into regional agencies but the quotes are 2-3 lakhs per region per campaign and take 3-4 weeks for delivery. She needs regional creatives for 5 markets by next month's product launch and has neither the budget nor the time.

### Secondary: Service Business Owners

**Rajesh — Founder of a 4-location dental clinic chain in Tamil Nadu**

Rajesh is opening his 5th clinic in Bangalore. He's been running ads on Instagram and Google in Tamil for his TN clinics — they work well. But Bangalore needs Kannada content with a completely different local flavor. He doesn't have a marketing team. He currently makes creatives in Canva with Google Translated text, and he knows it looks amateur. He needs something that feels native to Bangalore audiences but doesn't have the bandwidth to hire a local agency for a single clinic's social media.

### Early Adopter Profile

- Spending on digital ads but seeing diminishing returns in non-home regions
- Team of 1-5 people handling marketing (can't hire per-region specialists)
- Tech-comfortable (already using Canva, Meta Ads Manager, ChatGPT)
- Expanding from 1-2 regions to 5+ and hitting the localization wall

## Jobs To Be Done

**When** I'm launching a campaign targeting multiple Indian regions,
**I want to** generate culturally relevant creatives for each region from a single brief,
**So that** my ads feel local and drive higher engagement without needing a regional agency.

**When** I'm expanding my business into a new Indian state or city,
**I want to** quickly understand what resonates locally and create content around it,
**So that** my brand doesn't feel like an outsider in the new market.

**When** I have a working creative in one language/region,
**I want to** adapt it for other regions without losing the cultural nuance,
**So that** I can scale campaigns across India while maintaining quality.

**When** I'm a small business owner with no creative team,
**I want to** describe my business and get ready-to-post regional ads,
**So that** I can compete with bigger brands on local relevance.

## Solution

Admitra is an AI-powered creative agent that takes a brand brief and produces a complete hyperlocal creative package across Indian regions.

**What the user does:**
- Provides a brief (text description, a website URL, or brand assets)
- Specifies target regions or lets the agent recommend them

**What Admitra does:**
1. **Researches** — Understands the brand, analyzes target regions, identifies locally relevant cultural hooks (festivals, landmarks, local preferences, regional aesthetics)
2. **Strategizes** — Develops a campaign approach tailored per region, mapping the right tone, cultural references, and messaging angles for each market
3. **Writes** — Generates ad copy in regional languages (not translated — written natively) with proper cultural context, idioms, and local flavor. Includes transliteration for review.
4. **Designs** — Creates visual ad creatives (images and short videos) with region-appropriate aesthetics, colors, and imagery
5. **Delivers** — Outputs a structured creative package with platform-ready assets (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp) per region

**What makes it different from "just using ChatGPT + Canva":**
- It's not translation, it's cultural adaptation — the copy is written *for* the region, not converted *to* the language
- It knows what's locally relevant — not just the language but the landmarks, festivals, food, and cultural identity of specific towns and regions
- It produces complete, ready-to-use creatives — copy + visuals + platform formats — not just text suggestions
- It does this for multiple regions simultaneously from one brief, at a fraction of the cost and time of regional agencies
